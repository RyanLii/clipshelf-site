Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy covers the ClipShelf apps for Mac and iPhone, including the ClipShelf keyboard and share extension. In short: your clipboard history stays on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud account. We do not collect, receive, or sell any of your data.

What ClipShelf stores

  • Clipboard items. On Mac, ClipShelf saves what you copy. It skips content that apps mark as confidential or temporary (for example, from password managers) and content from apps you choose to ignore. On iPhone, ClipShelf saves the clipboard when you open the app or the keyboard (you can turn this off), when you tap the paste button, or when you share something to ClipShelf.
  • Pinboards and settings that you create.

This data is stored on your device. You can delete single items, clear your history, or choose how long history is kept in the app's settings. Deleting the app removes its data from that device.

iCloud sync

Sync is off until you turn it on. When it is on, ClipShelf stores your items and pinboards in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. Clipboard text is stored in encrypted fields. We cannot access your iCloud data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. To remove synced data, turn off sync and delete ClipShelf data in your iCloud storage settings.

Processing on your device

  • Text in images. On Mac, ClipShelf recognizes text in copied images so you can search for it. This uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Images are not sent anywhere.
  • Link previews. On Mac, ClipShelf can download the title and image of a web page you copied a link to. The request goes directly from your Mac to that website, as a browser would. You can turn link previews off in Settings.

The ClipShelf keyboard

The keyboard needs Full Access to read your ClipShelf history, which is stored in a container shared with the app. The keyboard only inserts the items you tap. It does not record or store what you type, and it does not use the network.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or servers of our own.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.

Children

ClipShelf is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact
