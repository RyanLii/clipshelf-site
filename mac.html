Clipboard manager for Mac

Everything you copy is one shortcut away. ClipShelf for Mac is in App Review and coming to the Mac App Store.

How to see clipboard history on a Mac

macOS has no built-in clipboard history. Once ClipShelf is running, it stays in the menu bar and saves what you copy from then on.

  1. Copy something in any app, as you normally do.
  2. Press Shift-Command-V. Your history opens at the bottom of the screen, newest first.
  3. Type a few words to search, or use the arrow keys to pick an item.
  4. Press Return to copy the item, then press Command-V where you want it.

Paste in order with Paste Stack

Filling in a form or moving several values? Press Control-Command-C, copy each item, then press Command-V to paste them one by one. You choose copy order or newest first.

Everything you copy, in control

  • Text, rich text, links, colors, images and files, with their original formatting.
  • Text in copied images is recognized on your Mac, so you can search for it.
  • Filter by type, app and date. Press Space for a full preview.
  • Confidential content from password managers is skipped, and you can ignore any app.
  • The shelf is hidden from screen sharing unless you turn that on.
  • Copying the same content again moves it to the top, so duplicates do not pile up.

Mac questions

Which Macs does it run on?

ClipShelf for Mac needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

How long is my history kept?

You choose: a day, a week, a month, a year or forever. Pinned items never expire.

How much does it cost?

ClipShelf for Mac is a paid app with a one-time price, no subscription and no in-app purchases. The price for your country will show on the Mac App Store.

Can I use it with my iPhone?

Yes. ClipShelf for iPhone is free. Turn on iCloud sync on both devices to share your history or only your pinboards.
