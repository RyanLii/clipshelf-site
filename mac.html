How to see clipboard history on a Mac

  1. Copy something in any app.
  2. Press Shift-Command-V. Your history opens, newest first.
  3. Type to search, or use the arrow keys.
  4. Press Return to copy the item, then Command-V to paste it.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shift-Command-VOpen the shelf
ReturnCopy the selected item
Command-1 to 9Copy item 1 to 9
SpacePreview the item
Command-EEdit the item
Command-NType a new item
Control-Command-CTurn Paste Stack on or off

Paste in order

Press Control-Command-C, copy each item, then press Command-V to paste them one by one.
