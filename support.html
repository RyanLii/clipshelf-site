Support

Help with ClipShelf for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

My items do not sync between Mac and iPhone

Turn on Settings > Sync on each device, and sign in to the same iCloud account. Use Sync Now to sync at once. With "Pinboards only", only pinned items sync.

The keyboard shows no items

In iPhone Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > ClipShelf, turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard needs it to read your ClipShelf history.

iPhone does not save what I copied

iOS lets apps read the clipboard only while they are open. Open ClipShelf or its keyboard after you copy, tap the paste button, or share the content to ClipShelf.

How do I paste on Mac?

Press Shift-Command-V, pick an item and press Return to copy it, then press Command-V in your app.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, system version and ClipShelf version.
