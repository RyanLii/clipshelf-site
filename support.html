Support

Help with ClipShelf for Mac and iPhone.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/RyanLii/clipshelf-site/issues. Please include your device, system version, and ClipShelf version.

Common questions

My items do not sync between Mac and iPhone. Turn on Settings > Sync on each device, and sign in to the same iCloud account. Use Sync Now to sync at once. With "Pinboards only", only pinned items sync.

The keyboard shows no items. In iPhone Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > ClipShelf, turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard needs it to read your ClipShelf history.

iPhone does not save what I copied. iOS lets apps read the clipboard only while they are open. Open ClipShelf or its keyboard after you copy, tap the paste button, or share the content to ClipShelf.

Direct paste does not work on Mac. Use the menu bar icon > Enable Direct Paste, and allow ClipShelf in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
